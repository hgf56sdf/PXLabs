**Choose a Topic:**

1. Some people believe that social media helps people stay connected and informed, while others believe it causes distraction and negatively affects mental health. Argue whether social media has a positive or negative impact on society.

2. Some people believe ambition is necessary for success, while others believe too much ambition can destroy a person’s morals and relationships. Argue which viewpoint you agree with more.

3. Some people believe that schools should focus mainly on academic achievement, while others believe that developing life skills is equally important. Argue which should be given greater importance in education.

4. Some people believe modern entertainment has reduced people’s interest in reading books, while others believe reading is still an important part of people’s lives. Argue whether reading is becoming less important in modern society.

5. Some people believe that students learn better through traditional classroom instruction, while others believe that online learning is more effective and flexible. Argue which method of learning is more beneficial for students.

### **Once you've chosen a topic, follow these steps to write your argumentative essay:**

---

### **Introduction**

* **Include**: General background, statement of the issue, thesis/opinion, brief mention of main reasons
* **Structure**:
  "In recent years, [general topic]. While some people believe that [opinion A], others argue that [opinion B]. This essay strongly agrees/disagrees with [main opinion] because [reason 1] and [reason 2]."
* **Tips**: Be clear, direct, 3–4 sentences, opinion obvious

---

### **Body Paragraph 1 (First Argument)**

* **Include**: Topic sentence, explanation, example, result/impact
* **Structure**:
  "Firstly, [main idea]. This is because [explanation]. For example, [example]. As a result, [effect]."
* **Tips**: Focus on ONE idea, explain clearly, use linking words like "Firstly," "In addition"

---

### **Body Paragraph 2 (Second Argument)**

* **Include**: Different supporting idea, clear explanation and development
* **Structure**:
  "Secondly, [new idea]. Furthermore, [explanation]. This shows that [result]."
* **Tips**: Do not repeat the first paragraph, build logical argument

---

### **Counterargument Paragraph**

* **Include**: Opposing opinion, reason people believe it, your response
* **Structure**:
  "However, some people argue that [opposing view]. They believe this because [reason]. While this may be true, [your response]."
* **Tips**: Shows critical thinking, keep short, always respond

---

### **Conclusion**

* **Include**: Restate opinion, summarize main ideas, final thought
* **Structure**:
  "In conclusion, I strongly agree/disagree that [topic]. This is because [main reasons]. Therefore, [final thought or suggestion]."
* **Tips**: Do NOT add new ideas, keep clear and strong

---

### **Word Choice (Vocabulary)**

* **Use**: Academic words like significant, beneficial, harmful, impact, topic-specific vocabulary
* **Avoid**: Very basic words like good, bad, very big

---

### **Sentence Fluency**

* **Do**: Use different sentence types, combine ideas smoothly
* **Example**:
  Simple: This is important.
  Better: This is important because it affects many people.
* **Tip**: Use connectors: because, although, therefore, however

---

### **Conventions (Grammar & Structure)**

* **Check**: Spelling, punctuation, capital letters, paragraphing
* **Tip**: Leave time to proofread

---

### **Final Tips for High Marks**

* Stay focused on the topic
* Support every idea with explanation
* Clear paragraph structure
* Write around 386 words
* Keep writing organized and easy to follow
